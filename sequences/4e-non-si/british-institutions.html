Article / document informatif

How does the UK work?

The United Kingdom is a constitutional monarchy: King Charles III is the Head of State (a symbolic role), but real political power belongs to the elected Prime Minister, who lives at 10 Downing Street. The Parliament has two chambers: the House of Commons (650 elected MPs) and the House of Lords (unelected, mostly hereditary or appointed peers). Laws are debated and voted there before being signed by the monarch — the « Royal Assent ». The UK is famous for not having a written constitution: it relies on tradition, precedent and key documents like the Magna Carta (1215) and the Bill of Rights (1689). In 2016, a referendum decided to leave the European Union — Brexit became official on 31 January 2020.

Questions de compréhension

  1. 1. Who is the current Head of State and where does the Prime Minister live?
  2. 2. How many chambers has the UK Parliament? Name them.
  3. 3. What is special about the UK constitution?
  4. 4. What was the Brexit referendum and when did the UK officially leave the EU?
